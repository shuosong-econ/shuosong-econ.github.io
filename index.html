01 / PERSONNEL RECORD

Shuo Song.

Economist. Researcher. Observer.

I study the systems
behind intelligence.

I am a PhD student at the Toulouse School of Economics, France. My research interests lie in empirical industrial organization.

My current work focuses on the industrial organization of generative AI: competition among large language models and the infrastructure behind them, including semiconductors and energy.

Before joining TSE, I obtained my Bachelor’s degree from the Wang Yanan Institute for Studies in Economics (WISE) at Xiamen University, China.
